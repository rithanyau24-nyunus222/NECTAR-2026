Your high-yield retirement compounder. Money here multiplies safely at 4.0% p.a. guaranteed.

MA 4.0%
Medisave Account (MA)

Reserved for hospital bills, MediShield Life insurance, and medical safety net. Earns 4.0% p.a.

THE SECRET SAUCE

The Extra 1.0% Bonus Interest Rule

The Singapore Government pays an extra 1.0% interest on the first $60,000 of your combined CPF balances (with up to $20,000 from your OA).

First $20k in Ordinary Account: 2.5% + 1.0% = 3.50% p.a.
First $40k in Special Account: 4.0% + 1.0% = 5.00% p.a.
📌 Golden Rule: Never drain your OA to $0!
